# Characters and past epics

The past can be huge. The present is fickle. These names exist so the present has scars.

---

## Marshal Sera Vohl (Hearthkin, present)

Best line-and-bomber officer of her Compact. Commands as if the shard is the universe — because for her, it is. She can win the globe you play and still lose the span without noticing. Tragic smallness, not incompetence.

People remember: she sleeps in her coat and names bombers after debts, not saints.

---

## Receipt-King Orun-Tal (Orunai, past)

Won a campaign so completely that no witnesses remained. Under Orunai law a victory without watching is weather. He **filed his own defeat**, set down the Unblinking Crown, and walked into a day-country that would not speak of him.

The Orunai still argue whether he is the greatest king or the most failed. That argument *is* his monument.

People remember: the empty filing hall, and the crown in a drawer labeled VOID.

---

## Archivist Yel (Mnari, living)

Spent her childhood to remember a war that had not happened yet. She is the span’s most accurate prophet and cannot recall her own name without a clerk. Mnari children cost memories; Yel paid retail.

People remember: she will tell you how your city dies, then ask who you are.

---

## Needle of Nine (Ravel, past)

Almost stitched two suns. Left a **dead color** in the sky that ships still steer around — a hue that does not occur in honest weather. The Ravel call it their proudest error. Local Open Tear risk, contained by superstition and by Wakeborn tolls that simply refuse that heading.

People remember: the color has no name, only a flinch.

---

## Mother-Kiln Halex (Kilnbound, present)

Canonized a battlefield by cooking it into a brick that still ticks. The brick is relic and weapon. She wants the Perfect Slag the way other people want a harvest: seasonal, inevitable, slightly boring to discuss.

People remember: the tick, heard through gloves.

---

## Drift-Speaker Quell (Wakeborn, present)

Collects tolls on the three busiest corridors. Has not set foot on a planet in eleven years. Owns no capital and more movement than most emperors. If the present has a “stronger hold,” it is often Quell’s, and it does not show on Hearthkin maps.

People remember: the invoice arrives before the ship does.

---

## The First Signature (Pale Clause, origin)

A ceasefire that refused sunset. Ink dried; the war did not. Limbs happened later. Every Pale Clause is an amendment of that one signing. There is no face, only a hand that still hovers over a line.

People remember: the date, which is still today.

---

## Green Account of Ilex (Velm, present)

A forest that learned to invoice. Largest contiguous living claim in the span that is not a city. Still not a winner. Sends polite seasonal bills to neighboring empires for shade, pollen, and the use of their dead.

People remember: the bills are fair, which is worse.
