# End-states

Several civilizational and cosmic failure modes. There is no single Big Bad. Structure: each people has a **game-set-match** identity — a way *they* win the universe — plus one **unowned** disaster anyone can cause.

These are lore-horizon victories. They do **not** replace live World Conquest F5 (land or zero enemy pressure) on the playable shard.

---

## Owned wins (legendary identity)

| End-state | Whose | What it feels like when it lands |
|-----------|--------|----------------------------------|
| **The Last Warmth** | Hearthkin | The lights go out except theirs. Travel starves. Charts remain; destinations do not. |
| **The Unblinking Crown** | Orunai | Every other people has attended. The ledger of witnesses is a single name. Quiet after the filing. |
| **The Closed Book** | Mnari | Yesterday will not vouch for you. Routes forget they existed. Empires become local rumors. |
| **The Single Thread** | The Ravel | Planets share a body. Borders become seams. Hearth returns as a draft, not a home. |
| **The Perfect Slag** | Kilnbound | The span cools into one useful brick. Life was the ore. Residue is the point. |
| **The Drowned Interval** | Wakeborn | Surfaces cannot pay distance. Capitals become museums with excellent weather and no visitors. |
| **The Binding Clause** | Pale Clause | Everything is still *allowed* that was already allowed. Nothing new is. Choice ends; calendar continues. |
| **The Rooted Age** | Velm | Worlds circulate. Empires discover they were organs. No surrender ceremony — just a season that does not end. |

No one of these is the “correct” ending. A melee admirer’s win (Unblinking Crown) is not higher-status than a corridor win (Drowned Interval) or a legal win (Binding Clause).

---

## Unowned failure: the Open Tear

If After is over-harvested, the Wake does not politely close. It **rips**. Cause arrives without effect. Armies march into days that will not keep them. Maps stay beautiful and stop being true.

Anyone can cause it. Several local tears already exist (see Needle of Nine; three corridors thinning in the present). The Open Tear is not a faction, not a god, not a personality. It is what the setting does when the past is spent like a mine with no roof.

Pale Clause cannot prosecute it (no precedent). Orunai cannot witness it (no stable day). Hearthkin keep sending fronts into it because fronts are what they have.

---

## Design rule

If a new species is added later, it needs **its own** end-state — not a coat of paint on Last Warmth. If a new apocalypse is added, it must not collapse the table into one villain.
