# The fickle present

Nobody has won. Someone will. The coin is on its edge.

## Who holds what (not the win)

| Hold | Who | What it actually means |
|------|-----|------------------------|
| **Mapped worlds** | Hearthkin | Strongest *charts*, including the live globe. Provincial. |
| **Movement** | Wakeborn (Quell’s corridors) | Strongest quiet grip. Does not look like a capital. |
| **Respect** | Orunai | Battlefields people still study. Empty filing halls they still fear. |
| **Records** | Mnari | Archives they are forgetting how to read. Yel remembers the wrong years on purpose. |
| **Living surface** | Velm (Ilex) | Biggest organism that is not a government. |
| **Residue / industry of waste** | Kilnbound | Rich in input, poor in friends. Halex’s brick ticks. |
| **Unfinished stitches** | The Ravel | Three seams everyone is afraid to finish (one is the dead color). |
| **Old wars still open** | Pale Clause | A majority of *ended* wars are, on paper, not ended. |

Hearthkin look strongest if you only count flags. Wakeborn look strongest if you only count arrivals. Both readings are true. Neither is a victory.

## Three thinning corridors

The Open Tear is not theoretical. Three Wake roads are paying more After than they earn. Wakeborn argue toll vs closure. Hearthkin keep scheduling convoys. Mnari have “forgotten” one of the three on purpose and will not say which.

## Playable now vs lore horizon (again, because it matters)

- **Now:** You play Hearthkin soldiers and bombers on one shard. Two banners. DESIGN.md locks. That war is real and small.
- **Horizon:** The table above. Unique wins. Interplanetary travel. Do not ship eight anatomies until art and systems exist. Do not pretend Azure vs Crimson is already Orunai vs Velm.

The present is fickle **because** the horizon is larger than the prototype. A Compact marshal can be a legend at home and a weather report abroad. Sera Vohl is winning a world. Quell is charging her for the trip she has not taken yet.
