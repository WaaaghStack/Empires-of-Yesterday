# Hearthkin Compact roster

**Status:** Exploratory lore. **Not** a live World Conquest lock. Custom Battle can field all eight jobs. Globe Play stays Hearthline (barracks) and Debt Wings (hangars).  
**Playable now:** the eight Compact jobs in Custom Battle; on the shard globe, **Hearthline** and **Debt Wings** only.

Azure / Crimson are still **banners**, not two species. Both Compacts use this list.

In-game: Main Menu → **Lore** → **Hearthkin roster**. Sprites: `assets/lore/hearthkin/`. Unit cards (groups + stats): [UNIT_METADATA.md](UNIT_METADATA.md).

## How they think about an army

Hearthkin believe a **front** is a line you can stand on. Logistics is the religion. After is spent like coal. A unit's job is a **receipt**: hold ground, collect a debt, file a claim against the sky.

They are slightly ashamed of wonder and of range-as-lying, so they still bring guns that reach and clerks who point them at aircraft — then they write an after-action that makes it sound like a line.

Prototype globe battles only spawn Hearthline and Debt Wings. Custom Battle can clock the other six in. That is why fifty rifles struggle on the globe: **Ceiling Clerks are not on that field.**

## Hearthline

The people of the mapped shard in a coat that stays decided. Rifles, a line, a front. Prototype **soldiers** are Hearthline under Compact paint.

They admire a decision that stays decided. They are poor at wars that are not fronts.

## Stovebreakers

When the line has found the door, someone has to go through the heat. Short heat-lances, heavier plates, ember vents. Hearthkin call this honest only because it is still a *front*, just a thicker one.

Slightly ashamed: it looks like Orunai honesty. They tell themselves it is logistics with a shorter receipt.

## Ash Wardens

Pioneers. Stakes, satchels, folding tools. They do not win the first minute. They make the fifth minute expensive to undo. "A place stays decided" is their liturgy.

## Walk-Mappers

Scouts. Map-cases, survey poles, binoculars. Hearthkin strength is **mapping**; Walk-Mappers are that strength walking. They find the line before the line exists.

Weak in a scrum. Essential to a Compact that thinks in charts.

## Ledger Pieces

Field guns. After spent at a distance. Range is a kind of lying, so the after-action calls it **accounting**. The shell is a line item.

## Rolling Hearths

Tracked hearths. Crates of After-coal, a stove that is also a chapel. Not a tank. If the Rolling Hearths go out, the Compact does not have an army — it has a hike.

This is the religion made visible.

## Ceiling Clerks

Anti-air. Clipboard and a tube that files claims against aircraft. They are why a mature Compact is not helpless against Debt Wings. They are **not** spawned from hangars or barracks. Globe fights still explain weak AA as missing clerks. Custom Battle can clock them in.

Bureaucratic on purpose. Hearthkin would rather invoice the sky than worship it.

## Debt Wings

Bombers. Sera Vohl names them after debts, not saints. They overfly, drop a blast, come around. Prototype **bombers** are Debt Wings.

The rest of the Hearthspan finds this adorable: a house fire with a flight plan.
