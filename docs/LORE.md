## What this is (and is not)

- **Is:** original interplanetary canon — many species, many ways the setting can end, sticky characters, a fickle present.
- **Is not:** a Warhammer, Star Wars, or Mass Effect clone with the serial numbers filed off. Mass Effect is a reference only for *capability* (many biologies, many cultures). Structure of “several apocalypses, each faction has a unique win” is the useful lesson from Total War: Warhammer legendary-lord identity — not their names, gods, or jokes.
- **Does not change** live World Conquest: still soldiers + bombers, still F5 win formula, still no roads/bridges.

## The one-sentence universe

The inhabited worlds are shards of a single ancestral planet that **aged apart**. Between them runs leftover Yesterday. Empires harvest it. The past was epic. The present is a coin on its edge. Someone will win. Nobody has.

## Species (sticky card)

| Name | Vibe | How they win |
|------|------|----------------|
| **Hearthkin** | Industrial fronts. Logistics as religion. Playable now. | **The Last Warmth** — monopolize remaining heat and After until only their hearths stay lit. |
| **Orunai** | Melee/directness as *honesty*. Violence that must be witnessed to count. | **The Unblinking Crown** — make the universe watch them win, then file the watching. |
| **Mnari** | Living archives. Children cost memories. Fragile, long. | **The Closed Book** — remember so completely that rivals cannot pay distance. |
| **The Ravel** | Craftspeople of unfinished worlds. They stitch the Split. | **The Single Thread** — reunify the span as a draft they never stop editing. |
| **Kilnbound** | Waste is holy. Furnace liturgy. Residue is proof. | **The Perfect Slag** — cook living worlds into one useful brick. |
| **Wakeborn** | They live *between* planets, not on them. | **The Drowned Interval** — flood the Wake so surfaces become isolated museums. |
| **The Pale Clause** | Agreements that learned to walk. Precedent with limbs. | **The Binding Clause** — the universe continues; choice does not. |
| **Velm** | A people and a crop. Ground that votes. | **The Rooted Age** — every world becomes one organism; empires become organs. |

**Unowned failure:** **the Open Tear** — overharvest of Yesterday. Cause without effect. Nobody wins. Any species can cause it.

## Present tense (fickle)

Hearthkin hold the most **mapped** worlds (including the globe you play). Wakeborn hold **movement** (corridors). Orunai hold **respect**. Mnari hold **records** they are forgetting how to read. Velm hold the largest living surface that is not a city. Nobody holds the win. Three corridors are thinning toward an Open Tear.
